## Work

Read [parallel ownership](../ROUTING-AND-PARALLELISM.md) before assigning a writer.

## Validation

Independent final reader verifies final repository commits, parity/exception evidence, affected tests, review-cap outcomes and original requirement dispositions. Confirm conditional results still apply to final inputs, especially A12/A13 check sources and A14 caller/threat assumptions. Separate completed, replaced/retired, conditional-no-trigger, blocked and unverified. Link one durable final result; do not duplicate all CI logs.

## Complete when

All issues are worked to a truthful disposition; no actionable unowned in-scope issue or unjustified byte gap remains. Conditional risks and owner decisions are visible. Final completion does not claim all 402 historical implementations were performed.

Historical task completion alone does not satisfy this current predicate. A conditional no-change result must include its evidence and trigger. A blocked prerequisite cannot be replaced with a success label.
