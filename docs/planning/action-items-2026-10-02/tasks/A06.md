## Validation

Do not hand-edit generated files. Use the retirement coverage map to select needed safety tests. Do not claim hostile competing-writer protection that the implementation cannot provide.

## Complete when

Historical task completion alone does not satisfy this current predicate. A conditional no-change result must include its evidence and trigger. A blocked prerequisite cannot be replaced with a success label.
